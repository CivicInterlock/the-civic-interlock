The Local Hub

Built by neighbors. Operated by the neighborhood.

You don't need a massive budget or a central authority to secure your neighborhood. You just need a clipboard, a shared space, and a commitment to trade honest work. Here is how you build the bypass.

How to start a local guild
1 Stock the Hardware

Gather a few neighbors, designate a shared space (a garage, pantry, or shed), and bring in spare tools, extra garden yields, and bulk goods.

System Impact:

This creates permanent physical collateral. Your community's safety net is backed by actual resources, not fiat debt.

2 Log the Equity

Use a simple paper ledger or a shared spreadsheet. When someone brings in items, credit their account with Resilience Units (RUs) based on the physical value they supplied.

System Impact:

This establishes a transparent, zero-interest accounting system. Your time and resources are tracked fairly without needing a bank to hold the score.

3 Trade the Labor

Neighbors post what they need and what they can offer. Fix a fence, prep a meal, or watch kids to earn RUs directly from each other. Then, spend those RUs at the storehouse.

System Impact:

Labor keeps wealth circulating locally. An hour spent fixing a roof today holds its value exactly, completely insulated from global inflation.

4 The Treasury Drip

As the trades happen, the ledger automatically drips a tiny fraction of a unit into a community reserve fund.

System Impact:

Everyday neighborhood friction pays for public works. You fund infrastructure repairs and community projects without a single tax dollar ever being extracted.
