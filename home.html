THE CIVIC INTERLOCK

Built by neighbors. Operated by the neighborhood.

The legacy economy is designed to extract wealth from your town and ship it to a server farm. The Civic Interlock is a mechanical bypass. It brings the power, the resources, and the control back to the local level.

> Base Layer Mechanics
1. Non-Inflation: Your Time Doesn't Rot In the legacy system, the money you save today buys less tomorrow because they keep printing more of it. In the Interlock, 1 Hour of Labor = 1 Hour of Resilience. An hour spent fixing a roof today is worth exactly an hour of electrical work ten years from now. No one can inflate away the value of your sweat.
2. Zero-Debt: No Interest, No Vampires The old economy runs on extraction, charging you compounding interest just for participating. The Interlock is a flat, zero-debt calculator. You trade the value you actually produce directly with your neighbors. No credit bureaus, no banks skimming off the top, and no debt traps.
3. Anti-Fragile: Air-Gapped Survival If a massive bank makes a bad bet on Wall Street, the global economy crashes. The Interlock is built on hard-capped, 150-person local networks. If the global supply chain seizes up, your neighborhood's ability to trade food, repairs, and labor doesn't blink. You are completely air-gapped from the chaos.
