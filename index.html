THE CIVIC INTERLOCK

The world has become incredibly capable at producing abundance.

Yet communities grow more fragile.

Not because we lack resources—
because we lack protocols for sharing them.

Civic Interlock is an attempt to build those protocols.

Not a government.

Not a revolution.

A civic operating system.
